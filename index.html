O₂ Odyssey · Module 2

Three mini-games

Twelve weeks of Dot's mornings, a hip rebuilt in a barn gym, and one guideline sheet fitted to three bodies. Each game runs in the browser, nothing to install. Best on a laptop or tablet in landscape.

Module 2 · Unit 1 · Game 1

Baker Street

Dorothy is seventy two, has never been active, and is pink in the face after five minutes of walking. Bronwyn is four years older and barely breathing. Play twelve weeks of Dot's mornings and find out what can still change, what cannot, and why chess goes better after a walk.

Module 2 · Unit 2 · Game 2

Remodel

Some months on. Dot walks the hill without stopping, and a new member the size of a barn door has joined the club. Derek used to play professional football. He says her legs are made of walking and it is time to make them out of something stronger. Three places, three decisions each: his barn, her hip, his knee.

Module 2 · Unit 3 · Game 3

The Fitting

The club's family Saturday. Derek has printed the national guideline on one sheet and pinned it on the bakery door, one copy for everybody. Dot's family has come: a daughter of forty nine, a granddaughter of twenty seven who is six months pregnant, and a granddaughter of fourteen who runs for her school. Three bodies, one sheet, and a grandmother who now knows better.

Play
